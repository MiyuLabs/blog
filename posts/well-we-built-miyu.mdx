## What Miyu Actually Is

Miyu is a small desk companion device. Think ambient night light meets lofi music player meets expressive desk pet — but without the app clutter, without the subscriptions, without the Silicon Valley wellness-washing.

It sits on your desk. It glows softly. It plays music. It has a face with animated expressions — it can look happy, annoyed, sleepy, or just idle. It reacts when you reach for it. It nudges you — gently, not intrusively — to take a break, drink water, or just breathe.

It has a face. Not a screen. A *face*. Expressive enough to feel alive, simple enough to not be distracting.

## Why Hardware

Software is easier. Everyone told me to build an app. But an app lives on your phone, which is also where your notifications live, and your social feeds, and everything competing for your attention. The whole point of Miyu is to give you something that *isn't* competing.

Hardware is harder. The supply chain is a puzzle. Manufacturing is expensive. The unit economics are brutal for a small batch. But it's also the only way to build something that genuinely sits in a different category from everything else on your desk.
